THE RULES OF EMPUJÓN

A little nudge goes a long way.

Two players. Eight pieces each. One empty 6 × 6 board. Terracotta starts.

  1. Place. Choose an empty square and place a piece from your supply.
  2. Push. All eight neighboring squares count, including diagonals. Each neighboring piece moves one square directly away, regardless of its owner. A piece stays put if another piece blocks its destination. There are no chain pushes.
  3. Return. Pieces pushed off an edge return to their owner's supply. Upgraded pieces keep their upgrade.
  4. Check. Resolve all pushes before checking the result. If both players win, the player who made the move wins.

Classic

Win with three consecutive pieces in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal line, or all eight of your pieces on the board.

Upgrade

Start with eight base pieces. A line of three of your pieces returns them to your supply, with all three base pieces upgraded. Mixed lines work too. The base piece still get upgraded. Upgraded pieces are selected by default when available. Use the piece selector to choose a base piece instead.

Upgraded pieces are stronger: base pieces cannot push them, even off an edge. Upgraded pieces can push both base and upgraded pieces, regardless of their owner. Occupied destinations still block pushes.

If all eight are on the board without a line, choose one base piece to return to your supply upgraded. You always have eight pieces total. For longer or overlapping lines, choose one triple to remove; separate lines each earn upgrades.

Win with three upgraded pieces in a line, or all eight upgraded pieces on the board.

An original presentation inspired by the mechanics of Scott Brady’s Gekitai and Gekitai 2.
